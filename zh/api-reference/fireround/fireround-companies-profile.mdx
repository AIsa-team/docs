Takes the company's domain or its id from companies/search. Coverage limit: Fireround tracks rounds announced from 2026 onward. It holds a few thousand companies, most of them startups that raised in 2026, and earlier rounds only for some of those. Most companies, and most rounds before 2026, are not on record, so a lookup of an arbitrary company's full funding history, its pre-2026 rounds, an investor's whole portfolio or acquisitions usually returns nothing here; use a company-data provider with historical funding coverage for those."
