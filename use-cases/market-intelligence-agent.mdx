## The problem

Investors, analysts, and strategy teams want a fast read on a company or event without stitching together five data vendors. The risk is a report that blends stale prices, unsourced claims, and speculation into one confident paragraph. A good outcome separates observed data (with timestamps and units), source-backed events (with filing or article links), and clearly labeled signals and scenarios.

## Prompt

Give this to your agent once AIsa is [connected via MCP](/agent-quickstart):

```txt wrap
Use AIsa to build a market brief on ticker <TICKER> for <time horizon, e.g. the last quarter>.
Find the right AIsa tools with `search` and quote paid calls with `get_details`. Ask me before any paid call, with the list of calls you plan to make.
Collect: current price snapshot, key financial metrics, the most recent 10-Q/10-K and its relevant items, recent company news, and any related prediction markets.
Record the timestamp and units for every number. Verify material news against filings or company releases.
Return sections: Observed data | Source-backed events | Signals (prediction markets, sentiment) | Interpretation | Risks and missing evidence | Data cutoff.
This is read-only research: do not place trades, change portfolios, or set alerts.
```

Variant for an event rather than a company: `Track <event> using prediction markets and news; label market odds as expectations, not outcomes.`

## APIs and Skills used

Through MCP, your agent reaches these via five tools at `https://mcp.aisa.one/mcp`: `search`, `list_categories`, and `get_details` are free; `use` and `batch_use` are billed per call.

| Step | What the agent calls | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current price | Last price, day change, timestamp | [Stock Price Snapshot](/api-reference/financial/get_prices-snapshot) |
| History | OHLCV bars for a date range | [Historical Stock Prices](/api-reference/financial/get_prices) |
| Fundamentals | Current ratio set | [Financial Metrics Snapshot](/api-reference/financial/get_financial-metrics-snapshot) |
| Filings | Filing index, then item text | [SEC Filings](/api-reference/financial/get_filings), [Filing Items](/api-reference/financial/get_filings-items) |
| News | Recent headlines with source and link | [Company News](/api-reference/financial/get_news) |
| Market signals | Polymarket and Kalshi markets | [Polymarket Markets](/api-reference/prediction-market/get_polymarket-markets), [Kalshi Markets](/api-reference/prediction-market/get_kalshi-markets) |
| Reusable workflow | Equity data, filings, estimates, macro | [MarketPulse](/agent-skills/marketpulse), [Trend Forecast](/agent-skills/trend-forecast) |
| Synthesis | A supported model, after retrieval | [Models](/guides/models) |

## Example output

<Note>Illustrative only. The ticker, figures, and links are placeholders, not real market data.</Note>

```md
## EXMPL (Example Corp): brief, data cutoff 2026-01-15 16:00 ET

| Observed data      | Value        | As of            | Source            |
| ------------------ | ------------ | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Last price         | <price> USD  | 2026-01-15 16:00 | prices/snapshot   |
| Gross margin (TTM) | <x>%         | 2026-01-15       | metrics/snapshot  |

Source-backed events
- 10-Q filed 2026-01-08; Item 1A adds a new supply-chain risk. [filing link]

Signals (not outcomes)
- A prediction market on "<event question>" trades at <p> implied probability.

Risks and missing evidence
- No guidance update found after the 10-Q.
```

## Guardrails

- **Separate data layers.** Prices and metrics describe what happened; filings and releases provide primary context; news is for discovery; and prediction markets and social chatter are signals, not facts.
- **Timestamp everything.** Record units, currency, and observation time. Do not combine values from different sources without checking definitions and frequency.
- **Label forecasts.** A scenario or forecast is never presented as a verified fact, and the output is not personalized financial advice.
- **Read-only by default.** Do not place trades, alter portfolios, publish, or trigger alerts unless the user explicitly requests that separate action and an authorized path exists.
- **Quote, then ask.** Get a free quote with `get_details`; pass an approved cap as `max_price_usd` where supported. See [Billing & Credits](/guides/pricing).
- **When AIsa may not be necessary:** a dedicated market-data provider is simpler if you need only one dataset and already manage the analysis, model, and billing layers.
