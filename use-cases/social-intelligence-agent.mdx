## The problem

Marketing, product, and community teams want to know how a launch, topic, or competitor is being discussed, and which creators are worth working with. Raw feeds are noisy, and a model left alone will happily invent sentiment, demographics, or trends. A good outcome is an evidence-linked brief: scope and time window, recurring themes with representative links, interpretation labeled as such, and the sampling limits stated. Posting or engaging remains a separate, approved step.

## Prompt

Give this to your agent once AIsa is [connected via MCP](/agent-quickstart):

```txt wrap
Use AIsa to analyze public discussion of <topic or product> on X, Reddit, and YouTube over the last <14> days, in <languages>.
Find the right AIsa tools with `search` and quote paid calls with `batch_quote`. Ask me before any paid call, and tell me how many pages of results you plan to fetch.
For every record keep: platform, canonical URL, author or channel handle, publication time, and the raw metrics returned.
Return: (1) scope and queries used; (2) 3–5 recurring themes, each with 2–3 representative links; (3) recurring questions or complaints; (4) your interpretation, labeled separately; (5) gaps and sampling limits.
Do not infer demographics, sensitive traits, or intent the sources do not state. Do not post, like, follow, or reply.
```

Variant for creator discovery: `Find 10 creators on YouTube or TikTok who cover <topic>, similar to @example_handle. Show handle, platform, profile URL, and why they fit. Leave out contact emails and AI-inferred demographic fields until I approve the shortlist.`

## APIs and Skills used

Through MCP, your agent reaches these via a few meta tools at `https://mcp.aisa.one/mcp`: `search`, `list_categories`, `get_details`, and `batch_quote` are free; `batch_use` (up to 20 calls) is billed per call.

| Step | What the agent calls | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X posts | Keyword search with advanced operators, Latest or Top | [Advanced Search](/api-reference/twitter/get_twitter-tweet-advanced-search) |
| X profiles | Public profile by handle | [Get User Info](/api-reference/twitter/get_twitter-user-info) |
| Reddit | Search across public subreddits | [Reddit Search](/api-reference/reddit/get_reddit-search) |
| YouTube | Videos, channels, playlists | [YouTube Search](/api-reference/search/get_youtube-search), [YouTube SERP](/agent-skills/youtube-serp) |
| Cross-platform brief | 30-day ranked, clustered brief with citations | [Last 30 Days](/agent-skills/last30days) |
| Creator discovery | Creators from a brief or a seed account | [Creator AI Search](/api-reference/waveinflu/post_waveinflu-ai-search), [Similar Creators](/api-reference/waveinflu/post_waveinflu-similar), [KOL Creator Discovery](/agent-skills/kol-creator-discovery) |
| Reusable X workflow | Read endpoints plus OAuth-gated engagement | [Twitter Autopilot](/agent-skills/twitter-autopilot) |
| Write action (gated) | Publish a post for the authenticated user | [Post Twitter](/api-reference/twitter/post_twitter-post-twitter) |

Use a Skill when the monitoring method is reusable. Call APIs directly when your application must control queries, pagination, deduplication, scoring, or storage.

## Example output

<Note>Illustrative only. Handles, links, and counts are placeholders, not real posts or accounts.</Note>

```md
## "Example App 2.0" launch: public discussion, last 14 days (EN)
Scope: X (Latest, 3 pages), Reddit (2 subreddits), YouTube (top 20 videos). Retrieved 2026-01-15.

Themes
1. Setup confusion around the new login flow: x.com/example_handle/status/000, reddit.com/r/example/comments/000
2. Praise for offline mode: youtube.com/watch?v=EXAMPLE000

Recurring questions
- "Does 2.0 still support exports?" (seen in <n> threads)

Interpretation (model, not source data)
- Early reaction skews toward usability rather than pricing.

Limits
- X results capped at 3 pages; no non-English sources; absence of discussion is not proof none exists.
```

## Guardrails

- **Purpose-bound collection.** Define the topic, entities, time window, platforms, and languages first. This is source-bounded analysis, not open-ended surveillance.
- **Preserve provenance.** Normalize timestamps and metrics if needed, but keep the original URL for every record. Separate source fields, derived metrics, model labels, and missing data.
- **Analyze cautiously.** Compare multiple records (and source types when possible) before drawing a conclusion. Missing results do not prove there is no discussion.
- **Privacy.** Collect only what the purpose requires, respect platform terms, avoid inferring sensitive traits, and never expose credentials or unrelated personal data. Creator search can return AI-inferred gender, age range, ethnicity, and contact emails; use those fields only when lawful and necessary.
- **Reading is not engaging.** Posting, liking, following, replying, or messaging requires OAuth and explicit approval: show the exact content and target, execute once, and verify the result. Treat automated engagement as a write action with reputational risk.
- **Quote, then ask.** Get a free quote with `batch_quote` and run the same calls with `batch_use` only after approval. A quote checks the estimate, not the final charge. See [Billing & Credits](/guides/pricing).
- **When AIsa may not be necessary:** a direct platform integration is simpler if you use only one source, already have stable authentication and compliance controls, and do not need models or Skills.
