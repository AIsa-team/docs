## The problem

Sales and partnerships teams spend hours building target lists, identifying the right contacts, and finding timely reasons to reach out. An agent can handle the research, but enrichment incurs costs, returns personal data, and CRM or outreach actions carry real-world side effects. A good outcome is a concise, scored list of accounts and contacts with source links and trigger events, plus draft messaging, with nothing written or sent until you approve.

## Prompt

Give this to your agent once AIsa is [connected via MCP](/agent-quickstart):

```txt wrap
Use AIsa to find <N> target accounts matching: <industry>, <headcount range>, <region>, <technology or signal>.
Find the right AIsa tools with `search` and quote paid calls with `get_details`. Ask me before any paid call, and especially before enrichment.
Step 1: search companies and people by criteria (no enrichment yet). Step 2: pull recent news for each account to find a trigger event. Step 3: score each account against my criteria and show the scoring.
Only after I approve a shortlist, enrich those people, requesting only name, title, and work email.
Return a table: account | why it fits | trigger event (with link and date) | contact role | missing or uncertain fields. Then a 3-sentence draft opener per account.
Do not create contacts, add anyone to a sequence, or send email.
```

## APIs and Skills used

Through MCP, your agent reaches these via five tools at `https://mcp.aisa.one/mcp`: `search`, `list_categories`, and `get_details` are free; `use` and `batch_use` are billed per call.

| Step | What the agent calls | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build a target list | Company search by headcount, industry, location, technology | [Organization Search](/api-reference/apollo/post_apollo-mixed-companies-search) |
| Find people | People search (contact details withheld) | [People API Search](/api-reference/apollo/post_apollo-mixed-people-api-search) |
| Trigger events | Company news by category and date | [News Articles Search](/api-reference/apollo/post_apollo-news-articles-search) |
| Company context | Website traffic snapshot | [Website Traffic Snapshot](/api-reference/similarweb/get_similarweb-website-traffic-snapshot), [Use Similarweb](/agent-skills/use-similarweb) |
| Enrich the shortlist | One company by domain; one person by identifiers | [Organization Enrichment](/api-reference/apollo/get_apollo-organizations-enrich), [People Enrichment](/api-reference/apollo/post_apollo-people-match) |
| Creator targets | Creator discovery for influencer programs | [KOL Creator Discovery](/agent-skills/kol-creator-discovery) |
| Write actions (gated) | Create a contact; add to a sequence | [Create a Contact](/api-reference/apollo/post_apollo-contacts), [Add Contacts to a Sequence](/api-reference/apollo/post_apollo-emailer-campaigns-sequence-id-add-contact-ids) |

People search indicates whether a match exists and flags if an email or phone number is available; enrichment reveals the details and is where the enrichment charge applies. Revealing personal emails or phone numbers is opt-in and billed extra.

## Guardrails

- **Read first, enrich later.** Discover broadly, then enrich only the approved shortlist. Do not enrich every result by default.
- **Data minimization.** Request only the fields the workflow requires; record the source and retrieval time; and comply with relevant privacy, consent, and outreach rules.
- **Inspectable scoring.** Keep source data, derived scores, model interpretation, and missing fields separate. The model must not invent revenue, headcount, titles, contact details, or buying intent.
- **Drafting is not sending.** Before creating a contact, adding someone to a sequence, sending a message, or updating a CRM: verify the connected account and permissions, show the exact action and target, obtain explicit approval, execute once, and verify the result. Read access does not imply write permission.
- **Quote, then ask.** Get a free quote with `get_details`; pass the approved cap as `max_price_usd` where supported. See [Billing & Credits](/guides/pricing).
- **When AIsa may not be necessary:** A direct CRM or data-provider integration is simpler if you use a single provider and already have stable enrichment, consent, and outreach processes.
