Recent news headlines for one stock: `title`, `source`, `date`, `url` and the echoed `ticker`, in a `news` array. `ticker` and `limit` are the only parameters — there is no full-text search and no date filter, so narrow by raising or lowering `limit` rather than by query. Use it for recent coverage of a company you have already identified. Headlines only: the article body is not returned, follow `url` for that. For the company's own filings rather than press coverage use [`get_financial_filings`](/api-reference/financial/get_filings).
