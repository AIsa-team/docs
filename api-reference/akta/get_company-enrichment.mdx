Enrich one private company across 16 sections and 75+ data points: firmographics, business model, competitive assessment, funding history, M&A activity, financial estimates, management, technology, and more. `company` accepts a website (`canva.com`) or a UUID from [`get_akta_company_search`](/api-reference/akta/get_company-search).

`sections` is a comma-separated list — you pay only for what you request:

| Section | Credits | Price | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|
| firmographic, business_model, company_assessment, technology, product_offering | 2 | $0.16 | company basics, GTM, moats & risks, tech stack, products |
| strategic_signal | 1.5 | $0.12 | scale metrics, partnerships, recent moves |
| management_profile, customer_profile, industry | 1 | $0.08 | executives, buyers, sector codes |
| trust_signal, company_hierarchy, digital_presence, financial_estimate, location | 0.5 | $0.04 | HQ/offices, subsidiaries, socials, revenue range |
| **funding_detail** | 3 | $0.24 | **rounds, investors, total raised, last round date** |
| **mna_and_investment** | 5 | $0.40 | **acquisitions and investments with dates** |

The workhorse for private-market research: start with `firmographic` for context, add `funding_detail` when the money trail matters, `mna_and_investment` for inorganic growth.
