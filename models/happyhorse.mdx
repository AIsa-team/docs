AIsa gives you access to HappyHorse video generation models with one AIsa API key. The model ID suffix names the input: `t2v` is text-to-video, `i2v` is image-to-video, and `r2v` is reference-to-video.

Video generation is asynchronous. Submit the job to `POST /v1/video/generations`, then poll `GET /v1/video/generations/{task_id}` until it finishes. See [Retrieve a video generation task](/api-reference/media-generation/retrieveVideoGeneration).

HappyHorse adds a "Happy Horse" watermark to the bottom-right corner by default. Send `"watermark": false` in the request body to turn it off.
