**Creator partnership planning for brands and agencies.** Find and assess creators, choose a partnership model, cost a feasible pilot, and prepare briefs, outreach drafts, and a measurement plan. Research runs through AIsa with a per-task spending allowance that you control.

<CodeGroup>

```bash AIsa CLI

```
</CodeGroup>

## Requirements

```bash
export AISA_API_KEY="your-aisa-api-key"
```

- Python 3.10 or later (standard library only)
- The skill runs its research through the bundled `scripts/request.py` helper, which calls AIsa's tool router with a fixed set of capabilities (no direct vendor calls).

## How research spending works

Every research call is quoted before it runs, and each task has a research allowance (the skill sets a small default; you can set your own or ask for a plan that uses only material you provide).

- If a quote exceeds the remaining allowance, the agent displays the quote and waits for your authorization before increasing the allowance.
- Each attempted call deducts its quoted amount from the allowance, even if it fails. Checking a quote alone is free and does not consume the allowance.
- There are no automatic paid retries, and the agent does not switch to a cheaper tool or smaller scope to bypass a pending approval.
- A quote is an estimate, not a hard billing cap. Charges are billed to your AIsa balance.

Research spending is separate from creator fees, product, shipping, and media budgets.

## Research capabilities

The helper exposes these fixed capabilities, called as `python3 scripts/request.py quote|use <capability> --arguments-file <json> --summary "<purpose>" --remaining-budget-usd-micros <n>`:

| Capability | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `similar` | Similar YouTube/TikTok creators by content direction, seed profile, or both (limit 1–10) |
| `email` | Business email for one selected YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram profile |
| `instagram-profile` | Profile for a known Instagram handle |
| `instagram-posts` | One page of recent posts for a known Instagram handle |
| `youtube-search` | One page of YouTube search results |
| `search` | Basic Tavily web search (up to 5 results) |
| `extract` | Content from up to 3 public HTTPS pages |

`quote` only fetches the schema and a quote; replace it with `use` to execute.

## When to use

- Creator sourcing, gifting, sponsorships, ambassador, and UGC programs
- Comparing creator proposals or planning a first pilot within a budget
- Drafting creator briefs, outreach, and a measurement plan

Example requests:

- "We sell portable coffee equipment in the US. Find five YouTube creators covering camping, explain their fit, and propose a three-creator pilot. Prepare outreach drafts for our review."
- "Compare these three creator proposals and recommend a four-week UGC pilot within our campaign budget. Use only the proposals I've provided."
- "Turn our occasional product gifting into an ambassador program: selection criteria, compensation options, monthly deliverables, and a 90-day evaluation."

The skill researches and drafts. It does not send messages, sign agreements, pay creators, publish posts, or change ad accounts. Similarity scores do not represent audience overlap or expected ROI, and a creator's region or language does not represent audience demographics.
